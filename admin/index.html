FAQ
ANEW CohoTrack — quick answers for instructors and coordinators.
Getting Started

Q: How do I log in?

A: Go to cohotrack.pages.dev/admin, enter the shared password, pick your name, then choose your role. Contact your program coordinator if you need the password.

Q: What is the difference between Instructor and Coordinator?

A: Instructors see a simplified dashboard with Gradebook, Study Cards, Tool Finals, and Demerits — the four things they use daily. Coordinators see the full hub including Attendance, Requests, Students, History, and Manage.

Q: I signed in as the wrong role.

A: Click Switch in the top navigation bar to go back to the role picker without having to re-enter the password.

Instructor Dashboard

Q: What does the Gradebook tile do?

A: Opens the gradebook for your class — enter grades, view student progress, and print reports. Your hub password works there, no separate login needed.

Q: What does the Tool Study Cards tile do?

A: Opens the study card page at cohotrack.pages.dev/tools/. Share that link with students before finals so they can review tool names and functions.

Q: What does the Tool Finals tile do?

A: Opens the test portal at cohotrack.pages.dev/tests/. Students sign in with their Google (Chromebook) account and your class PIN. You can also use the instructor PIN to preview the test and see answer keys.

Q: What does the Demerits tile do?

A: Opens the demerit form so you can issue a demerit or pull up a student record. Hit Back to Dashboard when done.

Tool Finals (Tests)

Q: What is the class PIN?

A: Each class has a unique 6-digit PIN that students enter along with their Google account to access the test. Coordinators can find each class PIN in the Manage tab. Instructors can ask their coordinator for it.

Q: How do I preview the test or see answer keys?

A: Go to cohotrack.pages.dev/tests/, click "Need guest access?", and enter the instructor PIN. Answer keys highlight in green as you go through each question.

Q: Where do I see student test scores?

A: Test results are saved automatically when students submit. Results view coming soon to the hub.

Attendance and Requests (Coordinators)

Q: How do student requests work?

A: Students submit requests at cohotrack.pages.dev. You get an email notification, then approve or deny from the Requests tab — email links are for notification only and never make the decision. The student gets an automatic email with the outcome.

Q: Where are pending requests?

A: Requests tab, pending items at the top.

Trouble?

Q: A student can't log in to the test.

A: Confirm they are using a Google account tied to a school Chromebook and that they have the correct 6-digit class PIN for your cohort.

Q: The gradebook says I am not assigned to this class.

A: Select YOUR instructor name from the name dropdown when logging in. If your name is missing, ask a coordinator to add you under Manage, Instructors.

Q: I need the password reset.

A: Contact your program coordinator — they control the shared password.
